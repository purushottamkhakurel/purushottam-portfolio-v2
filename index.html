01 / A LITTLE ABOUT MENot the full story. Just a starting point.

Curious about
how things work.
Serious about
why they matter.

I work in security assurance and GRC, helping organizations understand the security practices of the vendors they rely on. My work includes managing security questionnaires, reviewing policies and audit reports, checking evidence against responses, and coordinating follow-ups when answers need more clarity.

The value is in turning a large amount of security information into clear, useful findings: what is supported by evidence, where a gap remains, and what needs attention before a decision is made.

I’m continuing to build depth across vendor risk management, customer assurance, ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST CSF and privacy principles. Outside work, you’ll probably find me watching football, planning a trip or collecting ideas for the next thing to learn.
